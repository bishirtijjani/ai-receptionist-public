## 2. Paste-ready form answers

**"How will your app use instagram_manage_messages?"**

> AI Receptionist provides an AI-powered Instagram Direct Message auto-responder for medical spas and salons. A business connects its own Instagram professional account via Instagram Business Login. When one of that business's customers sends a Direct Message, our app receives it through the `messages` webhook, generates a helpful reply with an AI assistant (answering questions about services, hours, pricing, and booking), and sends the reply back to the customer on the business's behalf using `instagram_manage_messages`. The business can hand the conversation off to a human at any time. We use `instagram_manage_messages` solely to read incoming customer DMs sent to the connected business's own account and to respond to them.

**"Step-by-step instructions for the reviewer to test":**

> 1. On Instagram, open the profile **@example-spa.aesthetic.spa** (our demo business account, connected to this app) and tap **Message**.
> 2. Send: *"Hi, what services do you offer?"*
> 3. Within a few seconds an automated reply is delivered from the business account describing its services.
> 4. Send a follow-up: *"Are you open on Saturday?"* — observe a second automated, context-aware reply.
> The replies are generated by our app and delivered via the Instagram messaging API using `instagram_manage_messages`.
