# Med Spa Domain Research

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

Target the right buyer, not just any med spa.

**Fit:**
- Owner-operator OR practice manager of a med spa / aesthetic clinic
- Single location OR 2-3 location small group
- $500k - $3M annual revenue (Instagram follower count is a rough proxy: 3k - 30k followers usually fits)
- 3-15 employees
- Located in US, UK, Australia, Canada (English-speaking, USD-comfortable, WhatsApp friendly)
- 1-2 person decision (owner alone, or owner + practice manager)
- Active on Instagram for client acquisition

**Don't target:**
- Multi-location groups with 5+ sites (IT departments, procurement, long sales cycles)
- Big franchise med spas (LaserAway etc., already have call centers)
- Sub-$300k revenue spas (can't sustain $297/mo)
- Brand new spas pre-launch (no inquiry volume yet)

## Booking software landscape

Order of priority for integration support:

1. **Boulevard** (top tier, most professional med spas) - API: yes, good docs
2. **Vagaro** (mid-tier, single locations) - API: yes
3. **Mindbody** (older, fitness/wellness crossover) - API: yes, complex
4. **Aesthetic Record** (aesthetics-specific, growing) - API: yes
5. **Calendly** (smaller spas, consultations only) - API: yes, easy
6. **Square Appointments** (very small spas) - API: yes
7. **Booker** (older, declining) - API: yes

**For first 3 clients, support Boulevard + Vagaro + Calendly.** Add Mindbody + Aesthetic Record once you have 1 case study per system.

How to tell which one a spa uses: visit their website, click "Book now" or "Schedule." The destination URL or branding gives it away (`*.boulevard.com`, `*.vagaro.com`, etc.).

## Pain points (real, verbatim from forums and IG comments)

Use these as outreach hooks. Owners actually say:

1. "I'm answering DMs at 11 PM on my couch instead of being with my family"
2. "Half my Instagram DMs go unanswered for 24+ hours"
3. "We lose so many bookings to slow front desk replies"
4. "We hired a receptionist but she can't keep up with all the channels"
5. "I missed three consultations last week because the office didn't answer fast enough"
6. "Booking is the bottleneck. We have demand, we just can't capture it"
7. "Front desk turnover is killing us. Re-training every six months"
8. "Meta's auto-reply on IG is robotic and customers know"

The hottest hook: after-hours inquiry volume. Open with that.

## Common objections (have answers ready before the call)

| Objection | Response angle |
|---|---|
| "Customers don't want to talk to a bot" | Show the demo. Tone is indistinguishable. Customers prefer instant accurate replies over slow human ones. |
| "What about HIPAA?" | The bot handles inquiries and booking. Anything PHI-adjacent flags for human handoff. BAA signed as needed. |
| "We already use IG auto-replies / WhatsApp Business templates" | Those are templated. AI understands context. Side-by-side demo ends this objection. |
| "I want the personal touch" | Bot handles the 80% of routine inquiries so you get time back for the 20% of consults that matter. |
| "Setup sounds complicated" | 14 days. You give us services, prices, hours, booking access. We do the rest. |
| "What if it gives wrong prices?" | Prices and policies are hard-locked. Bot cannot invent them. Edge cases route to human. |
| "Why $1,497?" | Pays for itself with ~5 booked treatments. After that, every booking recovered is pure margin. |
| "I'd rather hire a receptionist" | Part-time evening receptionist costs $1,200-2,000/mo. Bot is $297, never sleeps, no turnover. |

## Where to find them

**LinkedIn (use without Sales Navigator first):**
- Boolean search: `("med spa" OR "medical spa" OR "aesthetic clinic") AND (owner OR founder OR "practice manager")`
- Filter by location (start with one US city)
- Connect with a personalized note OR DM directly if you can

**Instagram:**
- Hashtags: #medspa #medicalspa #medspaowner #aestheticnurse #botoxinjector #aestheticpractice
- Geo search: "Austin med spa," "Miami med spa," "London aesthetic clinic"
- Find via: search top posts in hashtag, look at profile, check bio for owner

**Google Maps:**
- "med spa near [major US city]" returns 20-50 results
- Pull: business name, website, IG handle (from website), phone, owner name (from About page or LinkedIn lookup)
- This is the fastest way to build a 200-prospect list in a weekend

## Geographic prioritization (your time zones)

Nigeria is UTC+1. Best overlap windows:
- UK / Western Europe: 9 AM - 5 PM their time = 10 AM - 6 PM your time. Perfect.
- US East Coast: 9 AM - 5 PM their time = 3 PM - 11 PM your time. Doable, evening pitches.
- US West Coast: 9 AM - 5 PM their time = 6 PM - 2 AM your time. Brutal. Send async, take calls in your morning.
- Australia: 9 AM - 5 PM their time = 1 AM - 9 AM your time. Send async, schedule calls for their 9-11 AM.

Best path: UK first (easiest time zone), then US East, then expand.
