# 15-Minute Discovery Call Script

## Goal

By minute 14, you have one of:
- Pilot signed ($1,497 setup + $297/mo)
- Founder's Pilot terms sent (case study application)
- Clear no, with reason logged
- Calendared follow-up at a specific date/time (not "I'll get back to you")

NOT acceptable:
- "I'll think about it" with no follow-up locked in
- "Send me more info" without a specific question
- A 45-min sprawling call that ends without commitment

## Pre-call prep (5 mins before)

- Pull their Instagram. Note: latest 3 posts, treatments they feature, follower count.
- Pull their website. Note: services list, prices if shown, booking software (from "Book now" link).
- Have the Loom open in a tab.
- Have a notes doc open. Pre-fill: spa name, decision-maker name, what they said yes to in email/DM that booked this call.

## Minute-by-minute

### 0:00 - 1:30 / Opening

Say:
> Hi [Name], good to meet you. Quick before we dive in, I'm Bishir, founder of AI Receptionist. I build AI receptionists specifically for med spas. This call is 15 minutes. I want to learn how you handle inquiries today, show you what we built, and see if it's a fit. Sound good?

Wait for yes. Move on.

### 1:30 - 7:00 / Discovery (listen 4x more than you talk)

Ask in order:

1. Walk me through what a typical Tuesday looks like for [Spa Name] from an inquiry standpoint. Where do people first reach out?
2. Roughly how many inquiries land after 6 PM in a week?
3. Who's answering them right now, and how fast?
4. When inquiries go cold, what do you think it's costing you? Just a gut number.
5. What's your booking software?
6. If a tool could handle every after-hours DM, book the easy ones, and flag the rest to your team, what would have to be true for you to actually trust it?

Question 6 is the most important. The answer is the objection you must clear in the next 6 minutes.

### 7:00 - 10:00 / Demo

Share screen. Play the Loom (first 90 sec is enough), or open the live demo bot.

After:
> That's the core flow. Any of those moments feel like something your team is dealing with?

If they nod:
> What's a real DM you got recently that you'd want to see the bot handle?

If you have your bot test environment open, demo it on their actual question. This is the highest-converting moment of the call.

### 10:00 - 12:00 / Pricing reveal + offer

Say:
> So here's how it works. $1,497 to build it, you're live in 14 days. After that, $297/month covers model costs, monitoring, and up to 4 hours of changes per month. 14-day money-back guarantee on the setup. Pays itself off with about 5 booked treatments.

Pause. Don't fill silence.

**If they say "that's reasonable":** go to close.

**If they hesitate / push back:**
> Totally fair. Two options. One, we keep the standard Pilot at $1,497 plus $297/mo. Two, you apply for the Founder's Pilot, which is free for 90 days in exchange for a public case study with metrics. Two slots left this quarter. Want to hear the terms?

**If they say "that's a lot":**
Walk the ROI math:
> If you book 5 extra treatments in month 1 at $300 each, that's $1,500, basically setup. From month 2 forward, every recovered booking is pure margin against $297. That's about 1 extra booking per month to break even on the retainer.

### 12:00 - 14:00 / Close

Say:
> Three ways to move forward. One, you book the Pilot today, I send a contract by EOD, we kick off Friday. Two, you want to apply for the Founder's Pilot, I send the terms now, you decide by Friday. Three, this isn't right for you, no worries.
>
> Which one?

Wait. Do not fill the silence.

### 14:00 - 15:00 / Lock the next step

**If yes (Pilot):**
- Send contract via DocuSign / PandaDoc right then while on the call
- Confirm kickoff date
- Add to your calendar
- Confirm you have their booking software access info coming next 24h

**If yes (Founder's Pilot):**
- Send terms doc right then
- Set deadline (Friday EOD) to decide
- Calendar a 10-min "decision call" for Friday

**If "need to think":**
> Totally fair. What specifically do you need to decide on?

Get the specific concern. Address if possible.

If unresolvable on the call:
> Want to schedule a 10-min follow-up Friday at 3 PM your time? I'll have whatever info you need ready.

Do not leave without a calendar invite locked.

**If no:**
> Out of curiosity, what's the actual block?

Listen. Note for future improvement. Don't argue.

> Thanks for being straight. Mind if I check back in 3 months?

Log them as `revisit` in your CRM.

## Objection-response table

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| Customers don't want bots | Totally get it. Show me how you'd test it. We can route the bot to your phone first, you approve every reply for 7 days, then turn it loose only when you're comfortable. |
| We already have IG auto-replies | Yeah, Meta's templated replies. Different thing. Want to see a side by side? |
| I'd rather hire a human | Makes sense. Part-time evening receptionist in [their city] runs $1,200-2,000/mo. Bot is $297, handles every channel 24/7, no turnover. Want to keep both on the table while we walk through this? |
| What about HIPAA? | Anything PHI is flagged for human handoff, never resolved by the bot. We sign BAAs as needed. Bot is essentially a smart receptionist that knows exactly when to stop. |
| How do I know it won't invent prices? | Two safeguards. Prices and policies are hard-locked to what you give us; bot can't make them up. And before go-live, we route every reply to you for approval for 4 days. You see exactly what it would have sent. |
| Setup sounds complicated | 14 days. Three things from you: service list with prices, booking software login, one 30-min content call. We do the rest. You see drafts daily. |
| I want to start small | The Pilot IS the small version. One spa, 14-day delivery, 14-day money-back. Nothing smaller actually delivers value. |
| I need to talk to my partner | Of course. Want me to send a one-page summary you can forward? Happy to hop on a 10-min call with both of you this week. |
| What if my customers don't use WhatsApp? | WhatsApp Business gets 95%+ open rates and most med spa clients already use it for booking. If your customer base is mostly DM-on-Instagram or SMS, we can route those channels into the same bot. |
| Where are you based? | AI Receptionist's HQ is in Nigeria. I run client hours during your business day and bill in USD via Wise / Grey. You talk to me directly, not a sales team. |

## What NOT to say

- "Hope you're doing well" (AI tell)
- "Touching base," "circling back" (sales-speak tells)
- "We're a Nigerian company" (irrelevant unless asked. Don't pre-flag a question they may not have)
- Em-dashes in your speech pace ("It's a, bot, that, answers"). Just say it plainly.
- Talking about how the tech works. They care about outcomes, not LLMs.
- Apologies for: your accent, the price, being a small team, anything. Be calm and direct.

## Mental frame during the call

- You're a peer, not a vendor. You've shipped 3 SaaS products; you know what you're doing.
- Their time is valuable. Your time is also valuable. The call is 15 minutes, not 45.
- The bot is built. The price is set. You are not negotiating against yourself.
- If they're a no, they're a no. Faster nos free up your time for yeses. Don't chase.

## After the call

Within 1 hour:
- Send promised follow-up (contract, case study terms, summary, or whatever you committed to)
- Log in spreadsheet: outcome, key quotes, objection theme, next step + date
- If next step is a follow-up, send the calendar invite NOW

Within 24 hours if they said yes:
- Welcome email with kickoff details
- Calendar invite for kickoff call
- Onboarding doc (services / pricing / booking access ask)
