# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-05-30*

## Product Overview

**One-liner:** WhatsApp AI Receptionist that books appointments, answers inquiries 24/7, and hands off to humans — built specifically for med spas.

**What it does:** Codeket's WhatsApp AI Receptionist connects to a med spa's WhatsApp Business number and acts as an always-on receptionist. It answers questions about services and prices, proposes available booking slots, collects intake info for new clients, and escalates to a human when needed (medical questions, complaints, discount requests). Configured per-client with hard-locked prices and policies — it cannot invent information.

**Product category:** AI automation / AI receptionist / conversational AI for service businesses

**Product type:** Productized service (AI agent + white-glove setup)

**Business model:**
- Paid: $1,497 setup + $297/month (Pilot)
- Founder's Pilot: Free for 90 days in exchange for a public case study with metrics (3 slots, filling now)
- Delivery: 14 days from contract to live bot
- Guarantee: 14-day money-back on setup fee

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** Med spa owners are losing bookings every day because inquiries go unanswered for hours — especially after hours, weekends, and during busy treatment windows. Every unanswered DM is a potential $300-1,500 booking that booked somewhere else.

**Why current solutions fall short:**
- **Manual front desk:** Can't monitor WhatsApp + IG + phone simultaneously. Goes home at 6 PM. High turnover means constant retraining.
- **Meta/IG auto-replies:** Templated. Customers know immediately. Can't answer a specific price question or propose a slot.
- **Generic chatbots:** Not trained on med spa services. Give vague answers. Don't know their prices, policies, or booking system.
- **Hiring an evening receptionist:** Costs $1,200-2,000/mo. Introduces turnover risk. Doesn't solve the channel problem.

**What it costs them (verbatim from forums and IG comments):**
- "I'm answering DMs at 11 PM on my couch instead of being with my family"
- "Half my Instagram DMs go unanswered for 24+ hours"
- "We lose so many bookings to slow front desk replies"
- "Booking is the bottleneck. We have demand, we just can't capture it"
- "Front desk turnover is killing us. Re-training every six months"
- "I missed three consultations last week because the office didn't answer fast enough"

**Emotional tension:** Guilt about missing messages. Stress about covering all channels. Fear of competitor who responds faster stealing clients.

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct:** Generic AI chatbot builders (ManyChat, Tidio, etc.) — fall short because not trained on med spa context, require significant setup, and produce robotic outputs that customers see through immediately.

**Secondary:** Virtual receptionist services (Ruby, Posh) — fall short because they're phone-only, not WhatsApp-native, cost $300-1000+/mo, and are slower than instant AI.

**Indirect:** Hiring a part-time evening receptionist — costs $1,200-2,000/mo, doesn't cover all channels, creates turnover risk.

**Status quo (most common "competitor"):** Owner or front desk answering manually — free, but costs evenings, weekends, and every booking that goes unanswered overnight.

---

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**
- Med-spa-specific: trained on treatments, prices, objections, booking flows — not a generic bot
- Hard-locked pricing: bot cannot invent prices or policies (the #1 objection is "what if it gives wrong prices")
- Human handoff built in: medical questions, complaints, and discount requests always escalate — HIPAA-aware
- 14-day delivery, white-glove: client gives services + prices + booking access, Codeket does the rest
- 4-day approval mode before go-live: owner sees every reply the bot would have sent before it's live
- Codeket founder (Bishir) is the direct point of contact — no sales team, no offshore delegation

**How we do it differently:** Per-client configuration at runtime. Every client's system prompt is their own services, prices, policies, and team — the bot literally cannot say anything that isn't in the config.

**Why customers choose us:** Speed to live (14 days), certainty that prices can't be invented, and the ability to talk directly to the builder.

## Switching Dynamics (JTBD Four Forces)

**Push (what frustrates them about the current approach):**
Answering DMs manually late at night. Missing bookings while treating patients. Watching front desk fall behind on multi-channel volume. Paying $1,500/mo for a receptionist who still misses messages.

**Pull (what attracts them to us):**
24/7 coverage without hiring. Instant response at any hour. Bot that actually knows their prices and services. 14-day delivery. Founder's Pilot (free for case study) de-risks the commitment.

**Habit (what keeps them stuck):**
"We've always just had [name] handle it." Fear of customers finding out it's a bot. Uncertainty about setup complexity. Not knowing WhatsApp Business API exists.

**Anxiety (what worries them about switching):**
Bot giving wrong prices. Customers reacting badly. HIPAA exposure. What happens when the bot fails — who catches it.
